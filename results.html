ACADEMIC TASK GUIDE

View Marks & Results

Follow these general steps to find your marks or academic results in the official portal.

Steps to follow

  1. 1
    Open the official student portal

    Use the portal link published by your institution.

  2. 2
    Sign in securely

    Enter your login details only on the official portal. Do not share your password.

  3. 3
    Go to the academic section

    Look for a menu such as Academics, Examination, Results or Student Services.

  4. 4
    Select the required term or examination

    Choose the semester, course or examination period if an option is provided.

  5. 5
    View your marks or results

    Read the information shown in the official portal and contact the appropriate office if you notice an issue.

Note: Menu names and result availability can vary. Confirm the current process with the official institution portal.
Open marks & results portal ↗

The official destination link must be added by the project team.

Keep your details private

This guide does not collect marks, grades, registration numbers or passwords. View personal results only after signing in to the official portal.
