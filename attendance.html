ACADEMIC TASK GUIDE

Check Attendance

Use these general steps to locate your attendance information. Exact menu names may differ between portals.

Steps to follow

  1. 1
    Open the official student portal

    Use the portal link provided by your institution or its official website.

  2. 2
    Sign in on the official portal

    Enter your credentials only on the official website. This guide will never ask for your password.

  3. 3
    Find the academic section

    Look for a menu such as Academics, Student Services or Attendance.

  4. 4
    Open attendance details

    Select the relevant course, subject or term if the portal asks you to.

  5. 5
    Review the displayed information

    Check the details in the official portal. Contact the relevant support team if information appears incorrect.

Note: These are general navigation directions. Confirm the exact route for your institution before relying on it.
