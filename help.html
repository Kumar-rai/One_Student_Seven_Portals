Help Centre

A few useful points to help you use this student task guide.

Does this website need a login?

No. You can read the instructions without signing in. If the official portal requires a login, sign in there directly.

Can I see my attendance or marks on this website?

No. This website only explains where to find that information. Personal academic records remain inside the official portal.

Why does the portal button need to be configured?

The project team must add and verify the correct official destination links for the institution before the website is published.

Is the search bar working?

No. It is a visual placeholder in this version and does not search or collect information.

What should I do if the steps look different?

Follow the latest instructions on the official portal or contact your institution's help or academic support team.

Safety reminder: Never enter your password on this guide or share it with another person. Use only official websites for account access.
